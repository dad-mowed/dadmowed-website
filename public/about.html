Our Story

Dad Mowed didn't start as a business plan — it started with one stay-at-home dad mowing a few yards on Pleasure Island. Word got around. Neighbors told neighbors. And what began as a way to help a few families with their lawns grew, referral by referral, into a full-scale lawn care company serving Carolina Beach and Kure Beach.

We're still built the same way today: on relationships, not advertising. Every client we have came to us because someone trusted us enough to send a friend our way.

Our logo started as a piece of original art. We asked our son, Ivy, to draw a picture of Dad mowing the lawn — he was 4 years old at the time, and this is exactly what he drew.

Artist: Ivy, age 4

Dad Mowed Lawn Care Services exists to give Pleasure Island homeowners a lawn care partner they can trust completely — showing up when we say we will, doing the job right every time, and treating every yard like it's our own.
